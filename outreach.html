What we've been up to

Our Outreach Programs

Every program below was designed and delivered by corps members, in partnership with local hubs and host communities across Uyo.

Home / Outreach

Training

📅 March 2026  •  Netizens ICT Hub, Uyo

Introduction to Data Analysis

Learn the fundamentals of data analysis and unlock opportunities in today's data-driven world.

Participants were introduced to spreadsheets, basic charting, and how to read data-driven reports — skills increasingly expected in entry-level office and research roles. The session closed with a walkthrough of free tools learners could keep practicing with after service members leave the community.

Workshop

📅 April 2026  •  Netizens ICT Hub, Uyo

Branding in Tech

Creating a unique identity and reputation that makes a tech professional, product, or company stand out.

The workshop walked young tech enthusiasts through the basics of personal branding — from a clear online presence to consistent visuals and messaging — and how that reputation compounds over a career or a growing product.

Market Outreach

📅 April 2026  •  Odua Ebot Market, Uyo

Market Digital Literacy Outreach

Sensitization outreach at Odua Ebot Market, where we engaged traders on online fraud prevention and internet safety.

Corps members went stall to stall across the market, teaching traders how to spot phishing links, protect their mobile money PINs, and recognise common online scams — practical safety habits for people who now rely on smartphones for daily business.

Workshop

📅 June 2026  •  Arise ICT Hub, Uyo

Basic Computer Literacy Workshop

Enlighting corps members with the knowledge to use computers effectively for learning, work, and communication.

Fellow corps members were taken through file management, typing fundamentals, and everyday office software — the kind of baseline computer confidence many had never had a chance to build before service year.

Training

📅 July 2026  •  Arise ICT Hub, Uyo

Creating a Single Paged Website Using AI

Corps members learn how to build and launch a professional, fully-functional single-page website using AI tools.

This very website was born from that session — a live demonstration of how far a well-prompted AI assistant can take a corps member with no coding background, from a blank page to a working, deployable site.
