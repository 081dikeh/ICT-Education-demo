Who we are

About ICT EDUCATION CDS

ICT EDUCATION CDS is a Community Development Service group committed to closing the digital divide in host communities. We partner with schools, teachers, market leaders, and local leaders to bring hands-on technology training to those that need it most, turning a one-year service year into a lasting foundation for digital opportunity.

Mission

To equip underserved communities with practical ICT skills that open doors to education, employment, and innovation.

Vision

A generation of learners and teachers confident in technology; ready to build, not just consume, the digital world.

Core Values

Service, integrity, innovation, and community; the same values NYSC calls every corps member to live out.

Our Objectives

Digital Literacy

Foundational computer skills for students and adults with little to no prior exposure to technology.

ICT Awareness

Demystifying technology and showing communities practical, everyday uses of ICT tools.

Community Impact

Measuring success not in sessions held, but in lives changed by lasting digital confidence.

Capacity Building

Training educators to confidently integrate ICT tools into everyday classroom teaching.

Innovation

Encouraging creative problem-solving with technology suited to local community challenges.

Coding Education

Introductory programming and logic-building sessions that spark curiosity in young learners.
